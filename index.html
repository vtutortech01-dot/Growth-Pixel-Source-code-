Questions

Before you message us.

Anything else, ask on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

Is the domain free?

E-commerce and real estate websites come with a free .com.ng domain and setup. Every other package includes free domain setup: you get the domain you want, and we set it up and connect it to your new website for you.

Can my customers pay me online?

Yes. E-commerce, real estate and church websites come with payment gateway integration, using a gateway such as Paystack or Flutterwave. Customers pay by card, bank transfer or USSD, and the money lands in your account.

Can I update my products or listings myself?

Yes. Real estate and e-commerce websites come with website analytics and a back office where you manage users, transactions, products and listings without a developer. Open the E-commerce or Real estate demo above and tap "Open the back office" to try it.

Will the website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is mobile friendly. Switch the demo above to Mobile to see exactly how your pages will look on a phone.

Why do prices say "from"?

The starting price covers the core build for that package. Extra pages, products or custom features change the final quote, and we agree on it with you before any work starts.
